  • take haunts and embers, constancy, spur on
  • haunts and embers a8, cascade to a5 a6
  • flip shattered fragments, take unlock + 2 energy (3 energy left)
  • constancy self (5 energy left)
  • spur on plays unlock (1 energy left)
  • set track element to animal
  • thresholded unlock gains + plays powerstorm
  • thresholded powerstorm self (4 energy left)
  • powerstormed thresholded tsac self (1 energy left)

  • twinned days self
  • twinned constancy self x2 (5 energy left)
  • powerstormed thresholded unlock gains + plays cast down (1 energy left)
